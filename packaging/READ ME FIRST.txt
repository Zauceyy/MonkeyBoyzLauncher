MonkeyBoyz Launcher
===================

INSTALL
1. Extract this zip first (right-click > Extract All). Do not run Setup from inside the zip.
2. Double-click "MonkeyBoyz Launcher Setup".
3. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" because the program is not code-signed.
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
4. Click Yes. A MonkeyBoyz Launcher shortcut appears on your Desktop and in the Start Menu.
No administrator password is needed.

PLAY
1. You need the normal Minecraft Launcher installed and opened at least once (signed in).
2. Export a profile in the CurseForge app (profile menu > Export Profile) and drop the zip
   onto the MonkeyBoyz Launcher window.
3. Press PLAY in MonkeyBoyz Launcher. The Minecraft Launcher opens by itself with the pack
   selected (if it was already open, it is restarted). Press Play there.

REMOVE
Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > MonkeyBoyz Launcher > Uninstall.
Your packs and worlds stay in %APPDATA%\MonkeyBoyzLauncher until you delete that folder.
